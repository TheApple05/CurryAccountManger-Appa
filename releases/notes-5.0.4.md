Adds Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3 Fabric ports to one update containing all seven supported Minecraft versions.

Choose the JAR for your Minecraft version and loader. Install only one account-manager build in an instance. The five older JARs are carried forward byte for byte from 5.0.3; the new 26.x JARs retain the token importer, encrypted account storage, and existing theme.

The 26.2 and 26.3 builds each passed 161 deterministic checks and 61 checks against the exact installable JAR in an isolated production client. Their validation files, sources, screenshots, and license are included in the attachments and version-specific bundles. Existing builds retain their original validation limits; regrouping does not claim new runtime testing.

Native Open TXT selection and successful live sign-in on 26.x remain unverified. TXT drag/drop and clipboard paste passed. One supplied refresh-token sample per 26.x build returned INVALID. Microsoft OAuth, TheAltening, multiplayer authentication, other mods, and Linux/macOS were not tested.

Original authorship and artwork belong to Melonik. These unofficial ports retain the GPL-3.0-only license. Version-specific source commits are recorded in release-manifest.json.
